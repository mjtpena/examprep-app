Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings (or the purchase screen), and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The app is crashing or not loading content

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the app — your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at no charge. Email us if the problem continues.

I found a question that seems incorrect

We continuously update our question banks. Please email us the exam code, question number, and the issue you found. We'll review and correct it in the next update.

Will my progress be saved if I reinstall?

Progress is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your data may be restored when you reinstall. Otherwise, progress will reset after a reinstall — but your purchase is always restorable.

I purchased the wrong app — can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple's instructions. We cannot issue refunds directly.

How often are questions updated?

We update question banks when Microsoft or Databricks publishes exam objective changes. Check the App Store for the latest version to ensure you have the most current content.

Is there a free trial or demo?

Some apps offer a limited number of free questions before purchase. Check the individual app description on the App Store for details.

What devices are supported?

All ExamPrep Apps support iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. All apps are optimised for both phone and tablet layouts.
