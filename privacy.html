Privacy Policy

Applies to all ExamPrep Apps including certifications from Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Dynamics 365, Databricks, Snowflake, AWS, CompTIA, Cisco, Kubernetes (CNCF), HashiCorp, Salesforce, PMI, ISC2, ISACA, and Linux Foundation.
Publisher: Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd (ABN registered in Australia)
Last updated: May 22, 2026

Summary: ExamPrep Apps do not collect, store, or share your personal data. All app content and progress is stored locally on your device. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

1. Who We Are

ExamPrep Apps are published by Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd, an Australian company. For privacy inquiries, contact us at: privacy@datachainconsulting.com

2. Data We Collect

These apps are offline-first exam preparation tools. We collect no personal data.

  • On-device only: Your study progress, bookmarks, quiz scores, and settings are stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • No account required: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.
  • No analytics: We do not use crash reporting, usage analytics, or behavioural tracking tools.
  • No advertising: We do not display ads and do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No behavioural profiling: We do not build user profiles or track behaviour across apps or websites.
  • No network requests: The apps function entirely offline. No data is sent to any server.

3. Purchases

These apps are paid apps purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive payment details, credit card numbers, or billing information. Purchases are governed by Apple’s Terms of Service and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

These apps do not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, social login providers, or advertising networks. The only external service interaction is:

  • Apple StoreKit — for validating your purchase (handled by iOS automatically)
  • iCloud Backup — only if you have iCloud backup enabled system-wide on your device; data is stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple’s privacy policy

5. Data Security

All data stored locally is protected by iOS device encryption. Since we do not transmit personal data to any external server, there is no risk of data breaches on our end.

6. Children’s Privacy

These apps are educational tools for adults preparing for professional certification exams. They are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access & deletion: All data is stored on your device. Delete it at any time by clearing app data in iOS Settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Portability: There is no data stored on our servers to export.
  • GDPR/CCPA: Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to disclose, delete, or restrict.

For any privacy-related requests, contact privacy@datachainconsulting.com.

8. International Users

These apps are available globally on the Apple App Store. Since no personal data is transmitted to our servers, there are no cross-border data transfers to disclose. The apps function identically in all regions.

9. App Store Privacy Label

In accordance with Apple’s App Privacy requirements, all ExamPrep Apps declare “Data Not Collected” — we do not collect any data linked to or not linked to your identity.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
